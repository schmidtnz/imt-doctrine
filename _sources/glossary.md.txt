# Glossary

Each term is defined once here, and pages link to it with `` {term}`POD` ``. Terms follow {term}`CIMS` usage where CIMS defines them.

```{glossary}
ACH
  Analysis of Competing Hypotheses. A structured method for testing scenarios against evidence, favouring the scenario least contradicted by it.

CIMS
  Coordinated Incident Management System. New Zealand's common framework for managing incidents across agencies.

CIS
  Close-In Search. A search of the immediate area around the initial planning point for the subject, clues and direction of travel.

COP
  Common Operating Picture. The single shared display of the incident: plans, maps, intelligence, resources and timelines.

DOT
  Direction of Travel.

ESP
  Extended Search Planning. The phase that follows the initial response when the subject has not been found.

FLO
  Family Liaison Officer.

IAP
  Incident Action Plan. The plan for one operational period: mission, objectives, strategies, tasks, factors and safety.

ICP
  Incident Control Point. The location from which the incident is controlled. "Information collection plan" is always written in full, never abbreviated.

IMT
  Incident Management Team.

IPP
  Initial Planning Point. The point distances are measured from, usually the LKP or PLS.

IR
  Initial Response. The first phase of a search, driven by pre-determined, statistically based actions.

LKP
  Last Known Point. The last location the subject is known to have been, from physical evidence.

LPB
  Lost Person Behaviour. Statistical data on how people in different subject categories behave and where they are found.

MBO
  Management by Objectives.

MMISSI TD
  Mnemonic for the initial response process: missing person report, map, information collection plan, scenario, search urgency, incident action plan, task, debrief.

MOST
  Mission, Objectives, Strategies, Tasks. The structure of an IAP.

MPR
  Missing Person Report.

OSC
  On-Scene Coordinator.

PLS
  Point Last Seen. The last location where someone saw the subject.

POA
  Probability of Area. The likelihood that the subject is in a given region or segment.

POD
  Probability of Detection. The likelihood that a search of an area would have found the subject if they were there.

POS
  Probability of Success. POA × POD for a searched area.

RCCNZ
  Rescue Coordination Centre New Zealand. Coordinating Authority for Category II operations.

SAD
  Search Area Determination. Setting the boundary of the search area.

SAR
  Search and Rescue.

SAREX
  Search and rescue exercise.

SAROP
  Search and rescue operation.

SEE
  Search Effort Evaluation. The team debrief that establishes coverage and POD.

SET
  Speed, Efficiency, Thoroughness. Tells a team which matters most for its task.

SMART
  Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. Criteria for objectives.

SMS
  Safety Management System.
```
