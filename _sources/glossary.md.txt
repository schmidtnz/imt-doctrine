# Glossary

Each term is defined once here. Pages link to it with `` {term}`POD` ``. Terms follow {term}`CIMS` usage where CIMS defines them.

```{glossary}
CIMS
  Coordinated Incident Management System. New Zealand's common framework for managing incidents across agencies.

ESP
  Extended Search Planning. The structured planning phase that follows the initial response when the subject has not been found.

IAP
  Incident Action Plan. The plan for an operational period: objectives, tasks, resources and safety.

ICP
  Incident Control Point. The location from which the incident is controlled. "Information collection plan" is always written in full, never abbreviated.

IMT
  Incident Management Team.

IPP
  Initial Planning Point. The point distances are measured from: the last known point or the point last seen.

IR
  Initial Response. The first, urgent phase of a search.

LKP
  Last Known Point. The last location the subject is known to have been, from physical evidence.

LPB
  Lost Person Behaviour. Statistical data on how people in different subject categories behave and where they are found.

PLS
  Point Last Seen. The last location where someone saw the subject.

POA
  Probability of Area. The likelihood that the subject is in a given area.

POD
  Probability of Detection. The likelihood that a search of an area would have detected the subject if the subject was there.

POS
  Probability of Success. POA × POD for a searched area.

SAR
  Search and Rescue.
```
