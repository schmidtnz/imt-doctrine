# PRN-04 Importance of regional collaboration

Groups meet proactively, share expertise and stay aligned with national standards, so they can call on each other quickly during operations. No team is isolated; all benefit from the collective experience of IMTs nationally.

**In short:** none of us is smarter than all of us.
