# PRN-14 Continuous improvement

The doctrine runs on a feedback loop. Every operation and {term}`SAREX` is debriefed for lessons and actionable insights. These improve local performance, then go to a national subject-matter-expert review point, and on to Police and {{ org }} IMT leads for ratification and a doctrine update.

The loop is driven by the sector stakeholders who live with the operational consequences.

**In short:** improve nationally, and let others learn from our mistakes and achievements.
