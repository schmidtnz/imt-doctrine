# PRN-13 Prioritising safety

Some risk may be accepted to save a saveable life, but the safety of {{ org }} personnel is non-negotiable. A robust safety culture includes dynamic risk assessment, fatigue management, thorough briefings, communications discipline and medical care for teams and the subject. Volunteers are only deployed on tasks they are trained and qualified for.

"Our people get to come home." "We are in the business of saving lives, not trading them."

**In short:** safety is an IMT obligation, not a word. It's their emergency, but our job.
