# CON-05 Leadership

{{ org }} works as a support agency to the Coordinating Authorities, who hold ultimate authority. Within that relationship, leadership is a cornerstone of a high-performing IMT, both within the team and between teams and agencies.

Strong leaders collaborate, communicate clearly, maintain situational awareness and seek expertise when needed. Leadership is developed through training, experience on the job and succession planning.
