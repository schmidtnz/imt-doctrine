# PRN-08 Evidence-based and intelligence-led operations

Operations rest on evidence and are guided by intelligence. Analytical methods give structure, complemented by the knowledge and intuition of experienced people. The same evidence-based approach applies to building doctrine, training, planning and deployment.

**In short:** use evidence and intelligence to support or refute our thinking and actions.
