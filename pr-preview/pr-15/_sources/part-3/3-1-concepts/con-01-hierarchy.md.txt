# CON-01 Hierarchical framework of the doctrine

The doctrine cascades from enduring principles to specific operational functions:

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| Principles | Foundational beliefs and intent behind the approach |
| Fundamental and core operational concepts | The central theories; the "how" |
| Guidelines | Recommended practice for a typical operation; not mandatory |
| Processes and procedures | Established sequences of action and SOPs |
| Systems | The frameworks that enable processes |
| Functions | Individual tasks within processes and systems |

The whole runs as a **double-loop learning system**: lessons found at the functional level flow upward and can change processes, guidelines, concepts and even principles. This site implements that loop. See {doc}`/how-this-works`.

## Proposed national IMT framework

A national framework would professionalise IMTs, give a career pathway and support succession:

- **IMT development pathway**: training and experience required at each level of responsibility.
- **National tutor pool**: tutors drawn from experienced, current IMT practitioners.
- **Regional representatives**: the subject-matter-expert collaboration point for consistent practice.
- **National IMT coordinator**: gives every group a voice and turns operational lessons into doctrine changes.

:::{note}
The level names in the draft text, its figure and the doctrine brief don't yet match. This site uses principle → concept → guideline → process → form.
:::
