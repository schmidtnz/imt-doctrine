# PRN-11 Systematic and methodical approach: POA × POD = POS

Operations aim to maximise {term}`POS`. In the initial response this generally follows the objectives of the Bike Wheel model: actions that statistically give the best chance of success. If the subject is not found, the operation moves to extended search planning, where areas with high {term}`POA` are searched in priority order with resources that give the highest {term}`POD`.

Focus on what can be controlled: intelligence gathering, a detailed subject profile, local knowledge and lost person behaviour data, and plausible scenarios that direct the search. Record every plan, action and decision. Proven processes support, but never replace, the IMT's judgement.

**In short:** focus on what you can control, trust the process, and work in small, well-considered steps.
