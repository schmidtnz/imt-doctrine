# PRN-09 Search urgency

The *decision to act* is urgent, even if the first action is only gathering information. Urgency never overrides safety or methodical planning: initial objectives avoid unnecessary risk and unproductive tasking.

The Coordinating Authority usually sets initial urgency shortly after notification. The IMT can review and formalise it during the initial response.

**In short:** decide urgently, then plan a response that fits the circumstances.
