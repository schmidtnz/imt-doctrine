# PRN-07 The critical role of communications

The overriding communications principle is **information superiority**: one reliable, integrated flow of intelligence to the IMT, giving shared situational awareness and enabling safe, coordinated deployment by all agencies.

**In short:** without intelligence coming in, the IMT cannot predict, plan, resource or oversee effectively.
