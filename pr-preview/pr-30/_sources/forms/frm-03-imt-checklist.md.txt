# FRM-03 IMT summary checklist

A rapid-reference record for people who know the process.

## Notification and safety

- [ ] Informant details recorded; MPR started
- [ ] Collaborating early, not planning alone
- [ ] Response decision made; teams on standby if not urgent
- [ ] Operations log, decision log and timeline started
- [ ] Safety plan started; safety officer appointed if needed

## Incident control point and information

- [ ] Location chosen (power, comms, space, scalable)
- [ ] Common operating picture set up
- [ ] IMT roles assigned, including family liaison and media
- [ ] Profile, information collection plan and enquiries under way

## Map and initial scenarios

- [ ] Planning points, close-in search area and route plotted
- [ ] LPB rings and theoretical distance plotted
- [ ] Hazards, attractants, barriers and corridors marked
- [ ] Quick scenarios ranked; most plausible set as an objective

## Urgency, IAP and tasking

- [ ] Search urgency confirmed; go/no-go for responder risk
- [ ] Operational period and mission set; SMART objectives, strategies and tasks
- [ ] Tasks of 4–6 hours with map, subject summary and hazards
- [ ] Teams briefed together

## Debrief

- [ ] Whole team debriefed; GPS, photos and maps reviewed
- [ ] POD recorded per searcher; clues analysed
- [ ] Results fed into the next IAP; POA updated

## Extended search planning

- [ ] IR reviewed; search area refined
- [ ] Regions and segments defined and weighted
- [ ] Intelligence gathering continues

## Handover, demobilisation and suspension

- [ ] Operational period summary complete; incoming IMT briefed
- [ ] Teams extracted and accounted for; welfare managed
- [ ] Hot debrief held; formal debrief scheduled
- [ ] If recommending suspension: evidence pack prepared (see {doc}`/part-3/3-3-guidelines/gdl-06-suspension`)
