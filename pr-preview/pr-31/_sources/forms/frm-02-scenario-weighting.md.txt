# FRM-02 Scenario weighting sheet

Use after the evidence has been presented to the whole IMT.

1. Each person independently gives their most likely scenario a score of 100.
2. They score every other scenario from 10 to 100, relative to it: 50 means half as likely. More than one scenario can score 100.
3. Add each scenario's scores.
4. Divide each total by the sum of all totals and multiply by 100 to get the weighting.

## Worked example

| Scenario | Member 1 | Member 2 | Member 3 | Total | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Injured near main track | 100 | 80 | 100 | 280 | 40 |
| B Side trip to a lake | 80 | 100 | 70 | 250 | 36 |
| C Lost in bush off track | 30 | 40 | 20 | 90 | 13 |
| D Sheltering off track | 20 | 30 | 30 | 80 | 11 |
| **Sum** | | | | **700** | **100** |

The weightings are relative values for setting priorities, written on the map as each region's POA.

*Method from draft v0.1, Part 3.1 §17. Example values are invented.*
