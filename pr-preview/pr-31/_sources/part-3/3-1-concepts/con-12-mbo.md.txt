# CON-12 Management by objectives

Management by objectives ({term}`MBO`) keeps every action tied to the mission, through short, time-bound operational periods. It is consistent with CIMS. Teams that jump straight to tasks usually have objectives in mind; writing them down creates clarity.

Remember **{term}`MOST`**:

| | Question |
|---|---|
| **M**ission | Why: for example "locate the missing person" |
| **O**bjectives | What will we do this period? |
| **S**trategies | How will we do it? |
| **T**asks | Who will do it? |

Objectives should be **SMART**: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound.

Short operational periods (often 8–12 hours, aligned with Coordinating Authority shifts) allow the plan to adapt to new information, manage fatigue, make progress measurable, and bring fresh thinking as IMTs rotate.
