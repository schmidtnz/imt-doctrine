# PRN-01 Unified and consistent approach

Every team, wherever it is, follows the same recognised practices and processes. Consistency makes large operations work, lets groups and Coordinating Authorities operate together, and gives a clear basis for national training, competencies and operational reviews.

Local groups bring resources and knowledge of terrain, community and weather. National doctrine, training, systems and assurance make that knowledge interoperable and scalable.

**In short:** set the principles once, apply them consistently everywhere.
