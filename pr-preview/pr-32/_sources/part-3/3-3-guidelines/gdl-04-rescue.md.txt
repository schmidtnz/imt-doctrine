# GDL-04 Rescue phase: known location

Rescue can start at any point, or as a stand-alone operation such as a beacon activation where the nature of the emergency is unknown until the party is reached. Finding the subject changes the mission but doesn't end the operation.

1. **Locate**: confirm position precisely, assess hazards, notify the IMT, protect the scene, prevent unnecessary convergence.
2. **Stabilise**: first aid, protection from the environment, reassurance, ongoing monitoring.
3. **Plan extraction**: walk-out, stretcher, rope, helicopter, boat or vehicle, matched to the subject's condition, weather, daylight, terrain and responder fatigue.
4. **Transfer care** with a structured medical handover, recording times, interventions and condition changes.
5. **Family and media** go through Police and the IMT.

If a fatality or crime is possible, preserve evidence, avoid unnecessary movement, record observations and follow Police direction.
