# PRN-02 Adherence to a national management framework (CIMS)

{{ org }} incident management aligns with the {term}`CIMS`, applied flexibly to the land SAR environment. Terms and acronyms follow the CIMS manual, with {{ org }}-specific terms only where needed.

CIMS is a scalable management structure. It does not say what actions to take, what the priorities are, or how to reason about the subject, environment and resources. That comes from SAR knowledge and experience.

**In short:** CIMS is needed for multi-agency interoperability, but it is not a SAR manual.
