# GDL-01 Managing an IMT: key actions

Carry these out continuously, from notification to conclusion, in every operation:

- **Develop the subject profile** through ongoing interviews and investigation.
- **Manage risk dynamically** until everyone is home after the final debrief.
- **Scale the structure**: apply {term}`CIMS` in proportion to the operation.
- **Prioritise the four Cs** in the initial response: confirm the planning point, close-in search, containment, communications. Immediate hazards or a strong scenario can take priority.
- **Document everything**: intelligence, decisions, resources, actions, debriefs.
- **Keep a common operating picture** on walls or shared displays, not hidden on individual screens.
- **Plan for a long operation** from the outset.
- **Engage help early**: catching up once behind is very hard.
- **Treat search teams as intelligence sources**: look for clues and sign, not just the subject.
- **Manage media and social channels** with a dedicated resource, usually through Police.
- **Use LPB data and the Bike Wheel** in the initial response.
- **Process before forms**: keep the method going even when forms or technology are unavailable.
- **Manage by objectives**: don't confuse being busy with being effective.

## Setting up

| Topic | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Notification | The Coordinating Authority is incident controller until it delegates. Use the MPR. If urgent, deploy now; otherwise put teams on standby and follow the initial response process. Avoid planning alone. |
| Safety | Develop a safety plan early. Appoint a safety officer if the environment is beyond the controller's expertise. Only send trained and equipped people. |
| Pre-plans | Check for and action any relevant pre-plan. |
| Incident control point | Choose a location with access, power, comms, display space and room to scale. Staff the IMT so no function is compromised. |
| Forward base | Decide its purpose first. Watch for isolated functions, loss of control by the IMT and intelligence that doesn't get passed on. |
