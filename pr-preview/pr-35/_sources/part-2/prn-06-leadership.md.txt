# PRN-06 Effective and supportive leadership

Leadership matters inside the team (cohesion and clarity) and between teams (trust and coordination with other groups and agencies). Good leaders collaborate, communicate clearly, keep situational awareness and seek expertise when needed. They empower team members to contribute.

**In short:** leadership affects operational outcomes; develop good leaders inside and outside the organisation.
