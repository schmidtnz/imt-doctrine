# CON-06 Incident management teams and CIMS

An IMT plans and manages the whole operation so it is safe, efficient and effective. An IMT should be set up for every operation, whatever its scale, so no one plans alone. Structure and formality scale with the operation: be practical at the start, then formalise roles as complexity and span of control grow.

| CIMS function | Role in a land search |
|---|---|
| Control | Overall strategy, objectives, safety and coordination; appointed by the lead agency. A response manager can run the IMT day to day. |
| Intelligence | What we know, what we need to know; subject profile, planning points, weather, terrain, field intelligence; builds the {term}`COP` |
| Planning | Where to search next; {term}`IAP`s, long-term and contingency plans |
| Operations | Tasking, coordinating and supervising field teams and air assets |
| Logistics | People, equipment, transport, food, facilities; responder wellbeing before and after tasks |
| Safety | Hazard identification and a safe work culture across all agencies |
| Welfare | Support for the subject's family and the affected community |
| Public information | Media and community messaging, usually with Police |

## Getting search teams to the right place

- Know the capability of local and neighbouring resources, and engage them early.
- Seek expert advice for unfamiliar tasks or resources.
- Review each operational period to check intelligence has been acted on.
- Use limited resources early to gather information.
- Use trained IMT members, and assume a protracted operation from the start.
- Work with local communities.

Diverse teams, with field, management and analytical backgrounds, make better decisions and avoid groupthink.
