# CON-10 Communications

Communications connect every level, from the subject to the incident controller. The IMT is the hub: it receives distress signals, status reports and clues, turns them into intelligence, coordinates internally, and sends a clear action plan back out. That feedback loop validates the strategy and lets it adjust.

The IMT needs clear, concise:

- briefings in a standard format, with safety included
- taskings with scheduled reporting times
- status reports and structured IMT meetings
- handovers to incoming IMTs
- radio or satellite communication with field teams
- planned debriefs of every team.

Field teams provide radio checks, situation reports on schedule, early intelligence on clues and conditions, and a POD from each searcher after the task.

| Capability | Doctrine position |
|---|---|
| Radio | Interoperable channels, disciplined procedure, fallbacks for terrain shadow |
| Mapping and GIS | One common picture of tasks, tracks, clues, hazards and probability areas |
| Drones and aircraft | Use where lawful, safe and relevant; don't overclaim effectiveness in dense bush |
| Mobile and digital data | Use Police-approved processes; treat digital clues as perishable and private |
| Data protection | Role-based access, minimal disclosure, secure storage |

Prefer simple, robust systems that work when infrastructure is degraded.
