# CON-11 Evidence-based and intelligence-led operations

- **Evidence-based**: choose strategies by appraising proven data (historical SAR statistics, research, professional expertise) rather than habit.
- **Intelligence-led**: continuously collect, process and analyse information during the operation, and use it to set priorities, adjust resources and change tactics.

Early in the initial response, statistics drive most actions. As the operation progresses, intelligence makes the strategy specific to this subject and this terrain. Record every plan, action and decision throughout.

The intelligence function turns information into intelligence under three tests: access, accuracy and actionability. Source reliability can be graded where people are trained to do so.

## Common operating picture

The {term}`COP` is the single, shared display of the incident: everyone works from the same verified facts, decisions are faster, and the IMT can see coverage and team positions at a glance. A SAR COP typically shows:

1. timeline (weather, subject actions, SAR actions)
2. current incident action plan
3. subject profile
4. information collection plan
5. weather and terrain overlays
6. planning map (planning points, hazards, attractants, clues, regions and segments, statistical rings, search effort)
7. operations map (team positions and status)
8. planning data (urgency, LPB category, scenarios, clues)
9. logistics and resource tracking.
