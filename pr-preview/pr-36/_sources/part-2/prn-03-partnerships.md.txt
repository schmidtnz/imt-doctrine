# PRN-03 Fostering partnerships and relationships

{{ org }} is a **support agency** to Police and {term}`RCCNZ` under a memorandum of understanding. The Coordinating Authorities hold authority and legislative responsibility; {{ org }} provides operational expertise, tactical advice, specialised people (including trained IMTs) and local knowledge. {{ org }} also supports other agencies where appropriate, for example in civil defence emergencies.

Diverse IMTs, with varied backgrounds and perspectives, avoid groupthink and produce more robust strategies.

**In short:** what our partnerships look like to us.
