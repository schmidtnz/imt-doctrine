# PRN-10 Structured information management

Built on the {term}`COP`, information management rests on three tests:

- **Access**: everyone who needs information can see it.
- **Accuracy**: intelligence is grounded in verified evidence; displaying it visually helps catch errors.
- **Actionability**: information is analysed and presented clearly enough to guide objectives.

**In short:** I need to see what you see. Record what you are doing and why.
