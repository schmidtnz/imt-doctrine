# PRN-12 The principle of simplicity

Processes must be simple to understand and easy to apply in real time. Operational tempo and experience vary widely across the country, so any complication should be refined into a simple process that keeps the underlying concept.

**In short:** keep it simple. It removes ambiguity and builds confidence.
