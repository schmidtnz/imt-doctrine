# GDL-02 Initial response phase

Follows the {doc}`initial response process </part-3/3-1-concepts/con-15-ir-process>`. Start an operations log, decision log and timeline straight away, and display everything on the common operating picture.

| Step | Guideline |
|---|---|
| 1. Missing person report | Complete or update the MPR. Build the profile and choose an LPB category. |
| 2. Map | Mark planning points, close-in search area, LPB rings and intended route. Analyse travel corridors, hazards, weather effects on terrain and subject, barriers and attractants. |
| 3. Information collection plan | Address the gaps from steps 1 and 2. Assign enquiries, record results, display key intelligence. |
| 4. Initial scenarios | Quick and obvious stories that point to a piece of terrain: start point, direction of travel, route, activity, destination. Rank by consensus. |
| 5. Search urgency | Confirm or update the Coordinating Authority's assessment. Consider go/no-go for responder risk: don't make their emergency your emergency. |
| 6. Incident action plan | Set the operational period and mission, consider the factors, write SMART objectives, strategies and tasks. |
| 7. Tasking | Specific, unambiguous tasks of 4–6 hours. State whether speed, efficiency or thoroughness matters most ({term}`SET`). Brief teams together in a standard format. |
| 8. Debrief | The most critical step. Debrief the whole team, not just the leader. Record POD from each searcher. Feed results into the next IAP. |

## Recording POD at the debrief

Ask each searcher for a rating, then confirm it as a number. Average the individual values; outliers show where re-searching is needed.
