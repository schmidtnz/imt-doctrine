# GDL-03 Handovers, demobilisation and operational debriefs

:::{note}
This section is empty in the draft. The points below come from the draft's summary checklist.
:::

## Handover

- Prepare the briefing area and update maps.
- Complete the operational period summary.
- The incoming IMT arrives early; the outgoing IMT briefs it using the IAP structure.
- The incident controller stays on briefly after handover.

## Demobilisation

- Set release priorities and plan safe extraction of teams.
- Keep communications until everyone is accounted for.
- Return equipment and complete sign-out.
- Look after welfare: food, warmth, rest, critical incident stress.
- Hold a hot debrief and schedule a formal debrief.
