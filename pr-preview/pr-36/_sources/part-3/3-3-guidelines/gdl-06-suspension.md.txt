# GDL-06 Suspension

:::{note}
This section is under review with the Coordinating Authority.
:::

Suspension is a Coordinating Authority decision. {{ org }} members provide input and advice but don't make it.

Options are a **limited continuous search** (flyers, media), **scaling down** to fewer assets in a smaller area, or **suspending** until new information comes to light.

An IMT preparing suspension evidence provides:

- a summary of all search actions
- a map of areas searched, coverage and gaps
- updated POA and POD analysis
- clues found and their relevance
- scenario analysis and weighting outcomes
- a specialist survivability assessment
- a risk assessment for continued searching
- a record of family liaison and the family's wishes.

The operation is then peer reviewed by someone not involved, the family is told, and approval is sought from the appropriate authority for the operation's category.
