# CON-03 Pre-operational readiness plans

IMTs develop and practise pre-plans for predictable events and locations, based on the area's SAR history. Pre-plans reduce the research and planning burden when an urgent response is needed. They can also cover how the IMT will manage civil defence responses or large numbers of spontaneous volunteers.

A pre-plan typically covers:

- activation and mobilisation
- the area, its geography, access points and typical activities
- initial actions and resources for common scenarios
- health and safety, and environmental conditions
- previous searches in the area
- communications plan and coverage
- key contacts, facilities, medical and logistics resources.

Keep pre-plans brief, and structure them consistently so people can find information quickly.
