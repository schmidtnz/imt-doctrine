# CON-16 The extended search planning process

Extended search planning builds a progressive plan on the results of earlier search effort. In outline:

1. Review the initial response (start while it winds down).
2. Determine the search area by subjective and deductive reasoning.
3. Develop scenarios that fit the evidence, LPB data and profile.
4. Mark scenario and geographical regions on the map.
5. Weight the regions.
6. Divide the highest-weighted regions into segments.
7. Weight the segments.
8. Task to the highest-weighted segments.
9. Debrief teams to establish POD.
10. Recalculate segment weights from POD and new intelligence.
11. Re-task to the next highest regions or segments.

Repeat steps 8–11. Because every segment carries a number, segments in different regions can be compared directly. The step-by-step version is {doc}`/part-3/3-4-processes/prc-02-extended-search`.
